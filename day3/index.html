About Bhubneswar

Bhubneswar

  • The region features a profound history spanning over 2,500 years,
    originating from ancient centers like Sisupalgarh and the nearby Mauryan rock edicts of Dhauligiri associated with Emperor Ashoka.
  • Together with Puri and Konark,
    Bhubaneswar forms the Swarna Tribhuja (Golden Triangle) of tourism in the state
